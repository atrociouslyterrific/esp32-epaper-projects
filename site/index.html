Pip acts out what the brain picks, as often as the brain picks it.

Pip is a squishy blob: it scoots to its bed to nap, its bowl to eat and its box to hide, and stretches and squashes as it jumps. The board does the same moves at e-paper speed (about 3 frames a second); here they're tweened smoothly. Click a small screen to make Pip do that action, or press BOOT or PWR to pet Pip, like on the real board.

What Pip says

Pip's voice is a tiny language model that writes new sentences one letter at a time, right on the board.

Example sayings appear once the voice model has trained.

Inside Pip's brain

Every sense and feeling on the left connects to every neuron in the next layer, 4,522 learned numbers in total. Each line is one real weight: teal pushes the next neuron up, orange pushes it down, and stronger weights draw darker. On the right, each action's circle grows with how often the brain currently picks it.
